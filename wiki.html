Getting started

  • You arrive at the Atrium, the sanctuary around the first validator. Nobody can build or fight there. Thea, its guardian, gives you your first objectives.
  • Mine a tree trunk by hand, turn the logs into planks, then craft a wooden pickaxe: stone can only be cut with a pickaxe.
  • Every block you mine becomes a token in your chest; every block you place remembers who placed it (tap it, or press I, to see its history).
  • The objective bar at the top of the screen shows what to do next, and the compass beside it points the way.
  • The world turns from day to night in 8 minutes. Nights are darker and more dangerous: a bed lets you sleep through them and heals you.

Chapters

The story comes in chapters: finish every objective of one to earn its Gwei and a title, and open the next. The objectives of a chapter can be done in any order.

Genesis +50 Gwei · a title

  1. Harvest wood — Mine a tree trunk, by hand.
  2. Craft a wooden pickaxe — Crafting (E): 3 planks. A log gives 4 planks.
  3. Mine an ether crystal — The blue ore in the rock. Stone is cut with a pickaxe.
  4. Pet an animal — Right-click (or tap) a friendly creature.
  5. Reach the City — Follow the validators path from the Atrium to the great diamond.
  6. Sleep in a bed — Place a bed, then use it after dusk: it rests you, and it becomes your home.

The Forge +100 Gwei · a title

Crystal answers to crystal. Forge a pickaxe from it, and the geodes deep below will open for you.

  1. Forge the crystal pickaxe — 2 planks + 3 crystals. Unique item, ×5 mining.
  2. Open a geode — Below layer 20, in the large caves. Only the crystal pickaxe opens them.
  3. Forge the pure ether pickaxe — 4 pure shards + 4 crystals + 2 planks.
  4. Forge a weapon — Craft a Volt Blade or a Pure Ether Blade to fight back.
  5. Defeat a hostile mob — Shadows lurk in caves, guardians watch dark validators. Fight one off.

The Awakening +200 Gwei · a title

Under everything lies the genesis rock. Break it, forge a validator heart, and give a dark validator its light back.

  1. Reach the genesis rock — Right at the bottom of the world, just above the bedrock. Only the pure ether pickaxe can break it.
  2. Craft a validator heart — 1 genesis fragment + 2 pure shards + 4 crystals.
  3. Relight an old validator — Follow the compass to the ruins, then right-click (or tap) the dark validator.
  4. Attest a validator — Objectives tab → Validators: visit one of your validators and attest it to collect its Gwei and keep it active.

The Network +400 Gwei · a title

One validator is a spark. A network is a fire. Relight more, defend the Atrium, and face the guardians of the towers and the depths.

  1. Relight three validators — One dark validator sleeps in each ruin. The compass in the Objectives tab points to the nearest.
  2. Trade with another player — Open Trade, offer something, and have them accept.
  3. Defend the Atrium — Every hour, for its first 10 minutes, corrupted nodes converge on the Atrium. Destroy one.
  4. Defeat the Plasma Core — Climb a tower past the wardens on each floor: the Plasma Core waits at the top, next to its treasure.
  5. Earn a Genesis Titan Fang — Defeat the Genesis Titan in the depths.

The Merge +600 Gwei · a title

Up in the sky floats a sanctum where work and stake were made one. Its twin guardians never fall alone: strike them down together.

  1. Climb to the Merge Sanctum — A floating arena far from the Atrium: the compass points to the nearest. Its Beacon Stair spirals up from the ground.
  2. Bring down the twins together — Proof-of-Work and Proof-of-Stake guard the sanctum. One struck down rises again after 12 s unless the other falls too.
  3. Wear the Merge Aegis — Open your chest, pick the Aegis, and equip it.

Circuits side chapter +100 Gwei · a title

When you have a moment: the Circuits Complex, east of here, has a working example of every gate trick. Copy them, then make your own.

  1. Light a lamp through a gate — Craft an AND, OR or NOT gate (Crafting, Circuits), then wire it with cable so its output powers a lamp.
  2. Build a two-key door — Wire two pressure plates into an AND gate, its output into a door: it only opens when both are pressed at once.
  3. Build a self-oscillating blinker — Loop a NOT gate's own output back into its own input with cable: no clock needed, it blinks on its own.
  4. Build a latch that remembers — Wire an OR gate: one input from a plate, another looped back from its own output, into a lamp. Tap the plate once — it stays lit. A working example stands in the Circuits Complex.
  5. Build a coin flip — Feed a self-oscillating NOT loop and a plate into an AND gate, its output into a latch. A quick tap lands on whichever phase the loop was in — too fast to call.
  6. Leave your mark: three gates — Keep an AND, an OR and a NOT gate of your own standing somewhere in the world (on your plot: the Circuits Complex is public, nothing can be built there).

Tools, weapons and gear

A better pickaxe mines faster and opens harder rock: the crystal pickaxe opens the geodes below layer 20, the pure ether pickaxe breaks the genesis rock at the bottom of the world. Unique items are numbered: each one is a single token with its own serial.

What you wear goes in the Character tab (E): a plating, a helm, gauntlets, leggings, an amulet and boots, each in its own slot, with what they add up to. Each kind of gear helps its own way, and the pieces add up: Volt is practical (faster mining, faster on your feet), Pure Ether hits harder with a blade, Genesis brings your hearts back sooner (70% sooner at most). Mix them as you like: everyone sees what you wear.

Validators and Gwei

  • Gwei is the game's currency, kept by the server. You earn it from chapters, mobs, the defense of the Atrium and, above all, your validators.
  • Dark validators sleep in the ruins (the compass points to the nearest). Craft a validator heart and relight one: it becomes yours and produces Gwei every 5 minutes, even when you're offline. Three validators at most per player: the other ruins are for the others.
  • A common validator earns about 120 Gwei an hour; rare, epic and legendary ones earn 2 to 8 times more. Upgrading adds 15% per level, up to level 10 (Validators tab).
  • Attest your validators at least once every 8 days (Validators tab): past that, they turn corrupted and stop producing until you attest again.
  • Every hour, for its first 10 minutes, corrupted nodes attack the Atrium: destroy them for Gwei, and a bonus when the network holds.

Plots

  • The world is cut into plots of 16 × 16 blocks. /claim shows the one you stand in: gold when free, green when yours, red when someone else's or public.
  • Your first plot costs 500 Gwei, then twice as much for each one you hold (1,000, 2,000, 4,000…), 9 at most per world. Giving one up lowers the next price; nothing is refunded.
  • On your plots, only you and the friends you invite can mine, build, open doors and chests.
  • The Atrium, the City and the Circuits Complex belong to everyone: nobody can claim them.

Chests and trading

  • A chest you place keeps its items on the server. On your plot, only you and your invites can open it; anywhere else, anyone can.
  • Each tower holds a treasure chest near its top, guarded by the wardens and the Plasma Core.
  • To trade, open the Trade tab (or /trade nickname): choose what you give and what you want; the other player accepts or not.

Hovercrafts

Ether Twin Diamond, Pure Twin Diamond, Genesis Twin Diamond, Radiant Twin Diamond, Ether Neon Glider, Pure Neon Glider, Genesis Neon Glider, Radiant Neon Glider. Hold one and right-click (on a phone: the hovercraft button) to get on board: twice as fast, over water too. A Twin Diamond has a back seat for a friend.

Shop and cosmetics

117 cosmetics: 27 outfits, 4 block packs, 6 tool trims, 1 validator skins, 9 trails, 8 combat trophies, 2 event trophies, 6 titles, 18 hats and helmets, 12 capes, wings and packs, 12 companions, 10 premium pieces (sold for euros), 2 emotes. Prices go from 180 to 25,000 Gwei, and a few picks are on sale each week. Press Try on any piece to see it on your character before you buy it. Trophies can't be bought: they're won in combat and during events.

Emotes

Gestures everyone around you sees: press G (or the smiley button on a phone), then pick one, or type its name in the chat. /wave Wave · /point Point · /cheer Cheer · /clap Clap · /sit Sit · /dance Dance (Workshop) · /bow Bow (Workshop) · /lie Lie down · /seat Sit down · /eat Eat. Sitting lasts until you move.

Your account

  • You enter with your nickname and a password. A free nickname makes your account; on another device, the same nickname and password bring your game there.
  • On your usual device the game remembers you. Account (in the menu) lets you change your password or log out.
  • Forgot your password? Write to contact@ethermines.org with your nickname and what you own in the game.
