A block world in Ethereum's colors, built with friends. Every block remembers who placed it: the world keeps a ledger of everything you build.

New here? Pick a nickname and a password: they're your account. Coming back on another device? The same nickname and password bring your game.

This password was given to you: choose your own to keep your game safe.

Fullscreen on iPhone: Safari's Share button, then "Add to Home Screen," and launch the game from the icon.
